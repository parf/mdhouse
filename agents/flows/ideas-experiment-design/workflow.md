# Design the smallest useful experiment

Authoring package only — not runnable. See the
[Shared execution contract](../README.md#runtime-boundary) for permissions, full commit markers,
failure recovery and future manual/scheduled execution. No live research or provider call occurs.

- Original idea/source and owner: `<original source links, IDs and responsible owner>`.

- Local destination declaration: `Plans/flows/ideas-experiment-design.md`.
- Source selection/provenance: `<actual supplied revisions, dates, audience and scope>`.

Record source references, original wording, assumptions, objections and unknowns separately. For
missing mandatory evidence, block its dependent claim; preserve independent work and rejected
alternatives. A report/proposal is not validation, an owner decision or an action approval.

## Domain steps

### hypothesis

Record the falsifiable idea hypothesis, supporting evidence, audience and owner. Distinguish a desired outcome from a known result and retain source references/objections.

### design

Propose the smallest validation, audience/cohort, exposure and duration needed for the stated question. Keep resource/privacy constraints and alternatives explicit; a plan does not launch or recruit for an experiment.

### measurement

Define success and guardrail metrics, baseline, numerator/denominator, period and source/collection plan before evaluation. Missing mandatory definitions/baseline block a success claim; do not invent a threshold or change it after seeing results.

### decision-rule

Predeclare the decision rule, owner and next action for success, failure or inconclusive evidence. Preserve uncertainty and reversibility; rules remain proposals until the actual owner approves the experiment scope.

### report

Return the experiment draft, assumptions, measurement gaps, risks and proposed decision rule. No observed success, causal effect or rollout follows from designing the experiment.

## Approval boundary and outcome

Launching a test, contacting participants, collecting new data, changing product behavior, spending resources or sending the plan needs a separate preview and explicit approval. Designing success/guardrail criteria is not execution or permission to move goalposts.

The local brief/report is complete without a tracker. A declared handoff is data only, not a sent
request; no source changes, roots, projects, experiments or scheduled runs are created by this package.
