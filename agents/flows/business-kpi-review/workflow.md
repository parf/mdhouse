## Domain steps

### define

Record metric definitions, targets, actuals, period, population, units, denominator and source freshness. Choose a weekly, monthly or quarterly readout cadence as an input, not an installed schedule.

### validate

Check comparable definitions/periods/cohorts, missingness, duplicates, aggregation and calculations against supplied inputs. Show formulas/denominators; missing mandatory comparable evidence blocks a target/variance conclusion rather than becoming a good or bad score.

### interpret

Separate observed movement from proposed drivers and causal inference. Cite supporting driver evidence, risks and uncertainty; correlation or timing alone is not a demonstrated driver. Distinguish proposed decisions from actual owner answers.

### report

Return the KPI readout with targets/actuals, validated comparisons, blocked comparisons, drivers, risks, decisions and follow-up proposals with owners. A complete report can retain unknowns without changing targets or claiming an operating decision.

## Approval boundary and outcome

Changing targets/definitions, source records, operating plans, assignments or publishing/sending a readout requires a separate preview and explicit approval. Descriptive calculations authorize no action or fabricated causal claim.

The default graph is analysis only; its local report is complete without a tracker. An unresolved
question or blocked comparison stays visible with the independent evidence already collected.
