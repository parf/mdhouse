# Compare and prioritize ideas

Authoring package only — not runnable. See the
[Shared execution contract](../README.md#runtime-boundary) for permissions, full commit markers,
failure recovery and future manual/scheduled execution. No live research or provider call occurs.

## Inputs and evidence

- Original idea/source and owner: `<original source links, IDs and responsible owner>`.
- Defaults: selected-document, explicit-baseline, product, idea-prioritization.
- Local destination declaration: `Plans/flows/ideas-prioritization.md`.
- Source selection/provenance: `<actual supplied revisions, dates, audience and scope>`.
- Required sources: candidate ideas and goal links, impact confidence and effort evidence or assumptions, urgency strategic fit reversibility and decision criteria.

Record source references, original wording, assumptions, objections and unknowns separately. For
missing mandatory evidence, block its dependent claim; preserve independent work and rejected
alternatives. A report/proposal is not validation, an owner decision or an action approval.

## Domain steps

### scope

Identify candidate ideas, goal links, decision owner and ranking criteria. Preserve original IDs and rejected alternatives; a prioritization request does not approve an idea or resource commitment.

### compare

Compare impact, confidence, effort, urgency, strategic fit and reversibility using supplied evidence. Expose unknown values and assumptions; do not turn unsupported claims into a numerical score or an apparently objective ranking.

### assumptions

Show which assumptions drive the comparison, what evidence would change it and which choices are reversible. Missing mandatory criteria/evidence blocks a definitive priority claim; a smaller validation can remain a proposal.

### report

Return the comparative evidence table, uncertainties, proposed next validation and pending owner choice. Preserve alternatives and do not hide assumptions in an opaque score or silently assign tasks.

## Approval boundary and outcome

Choosing an actual priority, allocating people/budget, changing roadmaps, assigning tasks or sending rankings needs a separate preview and explicit approval. The qualitative comparison approves none of those actions.

The local brief/report is complete without a tracker. A declared handoff is data only, not a sent
request; no source changes, roots, projects, experiments or scheduled runs are created by this package.
