# Prepare a source-backed request brief for counsel

Authoring package only — not runnable. See the
[Shared execution contract](../README.md#runtime-boundary) for execution and permissions.

## Inputs and evidence

- Authorized supplied documents, baseline and review owner: `<source references and owner>`.
- Defaults: selected-document, explicit-baseline, legal, counsel-request-brief.
- Local destination declaration: `Plans/flows/legal-request-brief.md`.
- Required sources: supplied facts timeline and document references, desired outcome and questions for qualified counsel, confidentiality disclosure limits and contradictory evidence.

Preserve source citations (document/version/clause/page), confidentiality limits, jurisdiction and
date uncertainty. Separate source facts, assumptions, proposals and uncertain interpretation.
For missing mandatory evidence, block dependent claims and request qualified human review; do not
invent applicable law, terms, dates, deadlines or permissions. No live research or provider access.

## Domain steps

### intake

Record supplied facts, documents, desired outcome, intended counsel and confidentiality boundaries. Keep jurisdiction and source date uncertainties explicit. A confidentiality label does not establish privilege or authorize disclosure.

### timeline

Build a source-cited timeline of facts, reported claims and contradictions. Distinguish creation, sending and receipt dates; missing records block dependent factual assertions, not the recording of a known conflict.

### questions

Draft questions for counsel about unresolved facts, jurisdiction, interpretation and desired outcome. Preserve contrary evidence; do not propose a legal strategy or decide liability from incomplete facts.

### brief

Assemble facts, timeline, source index, questions and desired outcome using minimum necessary disclosure. Separate releasable summary from restricted attachments. Privilege remains unconfirmed unless established by qualified counsel; do not imply a privilege guarantee.

## Approval boundary and outcome

Outputs are analytical drafts, not legal advice, unsupported legal conclusions or compliance
certification. Require qualified human review before interpretation or consequential action.
No filings, signatures, acceptance or sends occur. Those actions need separate preview and explicit
approval outside this package. The local report/register/brief is complete without a tracker.
