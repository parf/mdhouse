# Research and synthesize idea evidence

Authoring package only — not runnable. See the
[Shared execution contract](../README.md#runtime-boundary) for permissions, full commit markers,
failure recovery and future manual/scheduled execution. No live research or provider call occurs.

## Inputs and evidence

- Original idea/source and owner: `<original source links, IDs and responsible owner>`.
- Defaults: selected-document, explicit-baseline, product, opportunity-brief.
- Local destination declaration: `Plans/flows/ideas-research-synthesis.md`.
- Source selection/provenance: `<actual supplied revisions, dates, audience and scope>`.
- Required sources: original ideas and source references, related work evidence dates and objections, duplicate boundaries and provenance or explicit unknown.

Record source references, original wording, assumptions, objections and unknowns separately. For
missing mandatory evidence, block its dependent claim; preserve independent work and rejected
alternatives. A report/proposal is not validation, an owner decision or an action approval.

## Domain steps

### scope

Select the supplied ideas, decision question, audience and source boundaries. Record original IDs and source provenance; this authoring package performs no live research or provider retrieval.

### sources

Review evidence, related work, dates/methods and objections with source references. Missing mandatory provenance blocks dependent synthesis; do not substitute an unrelated source or omit inconvenient contradictions.

### cluster

Identify duplicates and propose clusters while preserving each note's original IDs, wording, audience and source links. Similar phrasing is not sufficient to erase distinctions; clustering neither deletes nor merges source files.

### synthesize

Produce an opportunity brief that separates observations, assumptions, related work and objections. Retain conflicting evidence and unresolved questions; agreement is not manufactured from different audiences or methods.

### report

Return the evidence/cluster map, short opportunity brief and next research proposals. Preserve rejected alternatives/history; report completion is not product validation or permission to contact sources.

## Approval boundary and outcome

Paid research purchases, participant contact, source-note edits/merges, assignments and publication need a separate preview and explicit approval. Ordinary source access still requires its authorized scope; this package makes no live calls.

The local brief/report is complete without a tracker. A declared handoff is data only, not a sent
request; no source changes, roots, projects, experiments or scheduled runs are created by this package.
