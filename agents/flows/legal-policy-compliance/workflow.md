# Compare a policy with supplied requirements

Authoring package only — not runnable. See the
[Shared execution contract](../README.md#runtime-boundary) for execution and permissions.

## Inputs and evidence

- Authorized supplied documents, baseline and review owner: `<source references and owner>`.
- Defaults: selected-document, explicit-baseline, legal, compliance-review.
- Local destination declaration: `Plans/flows/legal-policy-compliance.md`.
- Required sources: supplied policy or implementation evidence, supplied requirement set version jurisdiction and date, source mappings exceptions and interpretation uncertainties.

Preserve source citations (document/version/clause/page), confidentiality limits, jurisdiction and
date uncertainty. Separate source facts, assumptions, proposals and uncertain interpretation.
For missing mandatory evidence, block dependent claims and request qualified human review; do not
invent applicable law, terms, dates, deadlines or permissions. No live research or provider access.

## Domain steps

### scope

Record supplied policy, implementation evidence and requirement set, each with version, source citations, date, jurisdiction and confidentiality limits. Do not discover or claim current law from outside sources.

### map

Map each supplied requirement to policy text and implementation evidence separately. Record uncovered requirements, exceptions and uncertain applicability. Missing version/date/jurisdiction basis blocks dependent compliance interpretation.

### gaps

Separate observed text gaps from unverified implementation and uncertain interpretation. A policy sentence alone is not operating evidence; request qualified review of exceptions and ambiguous mappings.

### report

Return source-linked coverage, gaps and proposed verification steps. The comparison is not a compliance certification, legal conclusion or assertion that the requirement set is complete or current.

## Approval boundary and outcome

Outputs are analytical drafts, not legal advice, unsupported legal conclusions or compliance
certification. Require qualified human review before interpretation or consequential action.
No filings, signatures, acceptance or sends occur. Those actions need separate preview and explicit
approval outside this package. The local report/register/brief is complete without a tracker.
