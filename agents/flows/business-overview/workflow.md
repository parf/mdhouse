Authoring package only — not runnable. See the
[Shared execution contract](../README.md#runtime-boundary) for permissions, markers, failure
recovery and future manual/scheduled execution. No live research, provider calls or actions occur.

- Decision/report task and owner: `<owning task link and authority>`.

Record source references, dates/freshness, population/method, scope and limitations. Keep evidence,
assumptions, decisions and follow-up separate. For missing mandatory evidence, block its dependent
claim; explicit unknown/pending states never become verified facts or approved actions.

## Domain steps

### scope

Select the audience, project, reporting period and authorized source set. Inventory the product, customers, value proposition and current priorities; record explicit exclusions rather than expanding access.

### evidence

Collect supplied product/customer/channel and business records with source references and dates. Keep revenue model, costs and risks unknown when evidence is absent; product enthusiasm or a website claim is not verified commercial performance.

### synthesize

Explain customers, value proposition, channels, revenue model, costs, risks and priorities at the chosen reader depth. Separate observations, assumptions and open questions, and show how sources support each material claim.

### report

Return the audience overview, evidence index, unknowns and follow-up proposals. A useful overview can retain explicit gaps; it does not approve product positioning, pricing or a business strategy.

## Approval boundary and outcome

Changing product copy, pricing, budgets, published materials, customer records or sending a brief needs a separate preview and explicit approval. The overview drafts an explanation, not a commercial commitment.

The default graph is analysis only; its local report is complete without a tracker. An unresolved
question or blocked comparison stays visible with the independent evidence already collected.
