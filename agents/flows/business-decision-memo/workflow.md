Authoring package only — not runnable. See the

- Decision/report task and owner: `<owning task link and authority>`.

- Required sources: decision problem alternatives and evidence, cost risk reversibility and constraints, decision authority and actual answer or explicit pending.

Record source references, dates/freshness, population/method, scope and limitations. Keep evidence,
assumptions, decisions and follow-up separate. For missing mandatory evidence, block its dependent
claim; explicit unknown/pending states never become verified facts or approved actions.

## Domain steps

### frame

Name the decision, owner/authority, desired outcome, constraints and timing. Record whether an actual answer exists or the choice is pending; a memo request is not a decision.

### compare

Compare alternatives, evidence, costs, risks and reversibility with source references. Keep assumptions, rejected alternatives and unanswered questions distinct; unsupported certainty cannot justify implementation.

### recommend

State the recommendation and its rationale, trade-offs, conditions and next validation. A recommended option stays a proposal until an actual authorized owner answer is supplied.

### record

If an actual final decision is supplied, keep only the shortest current rule/necessary conditions in owning DECISIONS.md; preserve its full question, options, answer and rationale verbatim in owning RESOLVED.md with the same ID and anchored history links. If pending, leave the question open and do not fabricate a closing turn.

### report

Return the decision framing, evidence/options, recommendation, authority state and proposed next step. Recording an owner answer is distinct from applying edits or committing resources; retain the complete memo/history without a tracker.

## Approval boundary and outcome

Implementing a recommendation, committing resources, approving terms, changing decision/plan files or sending the memo requires a separate preview and explicit approval. The default graph records evidence and proposals; it approves no choice.

The default graph is analysis only; its local report is complete without a tracker. An unresolved
question or blocked comparison stays visible with the independent evidence already collected.
