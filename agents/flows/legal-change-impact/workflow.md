# Map a supplied legal change to possible impacts

Authoring package only — not runnable. See the
[Shared execution contract](../README.md#runtime-boundary) for execution and permissions.

## Inputs and evidence

- Authorized supplied documents, baseline and review owner: `<source references and owner>`.
- Defaults: selected-document, explicit-baseline, legal, legal-impact-map.
- Local destination declaration: `Plans/flows/legal-change-impact.md`.
- Required sources: supplied regulatory or contractual change and baseline, effective date jurisdiction and version evidence, affected products processes documents and owners.

Preserve source citations (document/version/clause/page), confidentiality limits, jurisdiction and
date uncertainty. Separate source facts, assumptions, proposals and uncertain interpretation.
For missing mandatory evidence, block dependent claims and request qualified human review; do not
invent applicable law, terms, dates, deadlines or permissions. No live research or provider access.

## Domain steps

### intake

Record the supplied regulatory or contractual change and baseline with exact source citations, version/date, jurisdiction and confidentiality limits. Do not fetch live regulations or treat a draft as adopted law.

### applicability

Identify effective date, transition terms, affected scope and uncertain interpretation for qualified human review. Missing mandatory applicability evidence blocks dependent conclusions; unknown does not mean applicable or exempt.

### impact

Map conditional impacts to products, processes, documents and owners. Distinguish observed baseline differences from assumptions about legal applicability; preserve unknown ownership and missing implementation evidence.

### remediation

Prepare draft remediation tasks with source, proposed owner, dependency and acceptance evidence. They remain proposals, without filing tickets, changing policy or deciding a compliance deadline.

### report

Return the conditional impact map, source comparison, uncertainties and draft remediation proposals. Only a qualified person may approve interpretation and consequential follow-up.

## Approval boundary and outcome

Outputs are analytical drafts, not legal advice, unsupported legal conclusions or compliance
certification. Require qualified human review before interpretation or consequential action.
No filings, signatures, acceptance or sends occur. Those actions need separate preview and explicit
approval outside this package. The local report/register/brief is complete without a tracker.
