# Draft an obligation and renewal register

Authoring package only — not runnable. See the
[Shared execution contract](../README.md#runtime-boundary) for execution and permissions.

## Inputs and evidence

- Authorized supplied documents, baseline and review owner: `<source references and owner>`.
- Defaults: selected-document, explicit-baseline, legal, obligation-register.
- Local destination declaration: `Plans/flows/legal-obligation-renewal.md`.
- Required sources: supplied commitments and renewal clauses, owners trigger dates notice windows and source locations, date timezone and confidentiality limits or explicit unknown.

Preserve source citations (document/version/clause/page), confidentiality limits, jurisdiction and
date uncertainty. Separate source facts, assumptions, proposals and uncertain interpretation.
For missing mandatory evidence, block dependent claims and request qualified human review; do not
invent applicable law, terms, dates, deadlines or permissions. No live research or provider access.

## Domain steps

### scope

Identify supplied agreements, selected baseline, authorized recipients and register owner. Preserve jurisdiction, source date and confidentiality limits without assuming current applicability.

### extract

Extract commitments, owner, renewal/termination terms, notice window, trigger and source clause. Mark an unassigned owner or missing trigger as unknown; distinguish document date from service or receipt date.

### windows

Show any proposed date calculation with its supplied trigger, window, calendar/business-day basis and timezone. If a mandatory basis is missing, record no deadline and block dependent calculation; do not assume notice delivery or receipt.

### register

Draft a maintained Markdown register with commitment, owner, source, trigger, notice window, uncertainty and next human verification. Optional reminders are future run configuration; reminders are not installed by this package.

## Approval boundary and outcome

Outputs are analytical drafts, not legal advice, unsupported legal conclusions or compliance
certification. Require qualified human review before interpretation or consequential action.
No filings, signatures, acceptance or sends occur. Those actions need separate preview and explicit
approval outside this package. The local report/register/brief is complete without a tracker.
