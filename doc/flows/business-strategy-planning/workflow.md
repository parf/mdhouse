Authoring package only — not runnable. See the
[Shared execution contract](../README.md#runtime-boundary) for permissions, markers, failure
recovery and future manual/scheduled execution. No live research, provider calls or actions occur.

## Inputs and evidence

- Decision/report task and owner: `<owning task link and authority>`.
- Defaults: selected-project, explicit-baseline, product, strategy-plan.
- Local destination declaration: `Plans/flows/business-strategy-planning.md`.
- Actual supplied period/source selection: `<source dates, definitions, paths and coverage>`.
- Required sources: goals constraints and decision criteria, option evidence costs and assumptions, owners milestones and measurable outcomes.

Record source references, dates/freshness, population/method, scope and limitations. Keep evidence,
assumptions, decisions and follow-up separate. For missing mandatory evidence, block its dependent
claim; explicit unknown/pending states never become verified facts or approved actions.

## Domain steps

### frame

Record goals, constraints, decision criteria, planning horizon and accountable owners. Separate the desired outcome from a prescribed solution; missing authority or required goal evidence blocks dependent commitments.

### options

Compare options, trade-offs, costs, risks, assumptions and reversibility from supplied evidence. Label effort/budget scenarios as assumptions; do not hide uncertainty in an unexplained score.

### milestones

Draft milestones with owners, dependencies, baseline/target, verification and measurable outcomes. Keep success and guardrail criteria explicit. A proposed sequence does not allocate people, change budgets or start work.

### report

Return the strategy options, recommendation, milestone proposal, assumptions, unresolved questions and next evidence needed. Preserve rejected alternatives in the decision history only after an actual owner decision; the draft is not approval.

## Approval boundary and outcome

Roadmap/status edits, resource or budget commitments, assignments, experiments and external stakeholder sends require a separate preview and explicit approval. Planning analysis does not authorize spending or execution.

The default graph is analysis only; its local report is complete without a tracker. An unresolved
question or blocked comparison stays visible with the independent evidence already collected.
