hypothetical planning inputs, not estimates or commitments for a real team.

## Input

The supplied goal is to reduce onboarding time with a constraint of 10 staff-days. A supplied
scenario places a focused how-to at 6 staff-days and a complete portal at 12 staff-days. Evidence
for either option's adoption effect is missing. An owner is named in the input but has not approved.

## Expected output
