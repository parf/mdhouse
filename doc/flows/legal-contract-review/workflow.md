# Review a supplied contract

Authoring package only — not runnable. See the
[Shared execution contract](../README.md#runtime-boundary) for execution and permissions.

## Inputs and evidence

- Authorized supplied documents, baseline and review owner: `<source references and owner>`.
- Defaults: selected-document, explicit-baseline, legal, contract-review.
- Local destination declaration: `Plans/flows/legal-contract-review.md`.
- Required sources: supplied contract versions and clause references, parties dates money obligations and missing terms, confidentiality jurisdiction and counsel review scope.

Preserve source citations (document/version/clause/page), confidentiality limits, jurisdiction and
date uncertainty. Separate source facts, assumptions, proposals and uncertain interpretation.
For missing mandatory evidence, block dependent claims and request qualified human review; do not
invent applicable law, terms, dates, deadlines or permissions. No live research or provider access.

## Domain steps

### intake

Record authorized contract versions, baseline, source citations and confidentiality boundaries. Identify the intended qualified reviewer and supplied jurisdiction/date evidence; unknown governing law stays unknown.

### extract

Summarize parties, dates, obligations, money, renewal and termination by clause/page. Separate exact source wording from paraphrase. Preserve unusual clauses, missing terms and conflicting versions without silently choosing one.

### issues

List ambiguities, conflicting notice clauses and questions for counsel. Block any dependent interpretation when required terms or applicability evidence are missing; do not determine enforceability or invent a resolution.

### report

Return a clause-linked summary, issue list and counsel questions, with dates, uncertainty and missing terms. A draft issue is not a legal conclusion or acceptance recommendation.

## Approval boundary and outcome

Outputs are analytical drafts, not legal advice, unsupported legal conclusions or compliance
certification. Require qualified human review before interpretation or consequential action.
No filings, signatures, acceptance or sends occur. Those actions need separate preview and explicit
approval outside this package. The local report/register/brief is complete without a tracker.
